# Concise writing and AI-pattern audit

Read the project writing guide and any artifact-specific rules first. Use the audience, purpose, medium and nearby content to judge each passage. The project guide governs ISO 24495-1, the adopted ASD-STE100 subset and normative wording. Do not claim full conformance from these heuristics.

Audit by default. Quote the smallest span that causes a concrete problem, give its location and suggest a specific fix. Treat uncertain intent as a judgment call. Rewrite only when requested. Preserve requirements, identifiers, locked terms, modality, conditions, negations, quantities, evidence, uncertainty, required structure and verbatim quotations. Flag missing facts or attribution.

Check for stock openings or generic endings, unsupported claims or vague attribution, empty modifiers, synonym cycling, and repeated framing. Keep repetition or contrast when it helps the reader understand a real distinction.

For slides, edit the source when asked. Use takeaway headlines and concise labels. Select details for the audience; preserve meaningful ownership, conditions and mechanisms. Report findings and suggested fixes, or summarize requested edits. Say when no justified changes were found.
